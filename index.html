Master thesis: worth the wait

With Nederlandse Spoorwegen. How many minutes is one level less crowding worth to a passenger, and what changes the answer.

I ran two focus groups, designed a discrete choice experiment around what passengers said, collected 9,108 choices from 759 people, modelled them in Python with generalised estimating equations and turned the result into rules a product team can build.

10.3 min Willingness to wait when deciding on the platform (95% CI 9.2 to 11.5)
14.2 min The same trade off when deciding at home (95% CI 12.4 to 15.9)
41.3% Share of all choices where passengers waited instead of boarding
